How is this calculated?
  • Activity (25%): Evaluates commit recency (days since latest commit) and push cadence. Active commits in the last 7 days receive top scores.
  • Issue Management (20%): Calculated as Resolution Rate = (Closed Issues / Total Issues Analyzed) × 100, factoring in unresolved issues aged >90 days.
  • PR Activity (20%): Calculated as Merge Rate = (Merged PRs / Total PRs Analyzed) × 100, factoring in review turnaround duration.
  • Contributor Diversity (15%): Analyzes contributor volume and contribution distribution concentration (resilience against maintainer churn).
  • Maintenance & Hygiene (20%): Validates official open-source license presence, project purpose description, and non-archived status.
